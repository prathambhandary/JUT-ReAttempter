Your Option: 3

Correct Option: 3

Score:4/-1 1.00

Detailed Answer: The Debye, Huckel and Onsager equation gives the variation of molar conductivity (Λm) with concentration (C) as, Λm = Λm ⁰ - A√C

⇒ λC = λ∞- B√C       Where, B is a positive constant.

Question No:40

In an experiment, the specific conductivity (κ) of a 0.1 M solution of a weak monobasic acid is found to be 'x' S cm⁻¹. If the solution is diluted to 0.01 M, its specific conductivity becomes 'y' S cm⁻¹. Which of the following inequalities correctly describes the relationship between x and y?

1 ) x = 10y   2 ) x > 10y
3 ) y < x < 10y 4 ) x < y

Your Option: 2

Correct Option: 3

Score:4/-1 0.00

Detailed Answer: When a weak electrolyte solution is diluted by a factor of 10 (from 0.1 M to 0.01 M), two opposing factors influence specific conductivity (κ):
1. The volume increases 10-fold, which would ideally make the ion density and κ decrease exactly 10 times (making x = 10y).
2. According to dilution law, dilution increases the degree of dissociation (α), meaning *more* total ions are generated than before.
Because new ions are created, the specific conductivity does not drop by a full factor of 10. Therefore, the new conductivity 'y' is greater than x/10 (meaning 10y > x). At the same time, because it is diluted, the absolute ion concentration per cm³ is still less than the concentrated version, so x > y.
Combining these gives: y < x < 10y.

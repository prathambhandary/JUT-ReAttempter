1 ) as elastic potential energy completely 2 ) as thermal energy completely
3 ) half as elastic potential energy and half has thermal energy 4 ) as kinetic energy of the load completely

Your Option: 1

Correct Option: 3

Score:4/-1 0.00

Detailed Answer: When a metal wire elongates by hanging a load Mg on it, decrease in potential energy of the load = M g l where L = elongation of metal wire.

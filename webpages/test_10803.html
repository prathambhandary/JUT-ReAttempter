Your Option: 4

Correct Option: 4

Score:4/-1 4.00

Detailed Answer: Plasmodium parasite enters the human bloodstream, it first multiplies inside liver cells and then moves into the red blood cells (RBCs). Inside the RBCs, the parasite feeds on hemoglobin, producing a toxic crystalline byproduct called haemozoin.

Question No:45

Coordination isomers differ in:

1 ) The arrangement of ligands around metal      2 ) The metal ions themselves
3 ) The ligands in the coordination sphere of each metal        4 ) Both b and c

Your Option: 4

Correct Option: 4

Score:4/-1 1.00

Detailed Answer: Coordination isomers occur when a compound contains complex cations and complex anions, and these
isomers differ by the interchange of ligands between the different metal ions or by swapping metal ions themselves in the coordination spheres. Specifically, the ligands directly attached (in the coordination sphere) to the metal ions differ between coordination isomers, and sometimes the metals involved may also differ in their position within the overall compound. They do not differ simply by the arrangement of ligands around the same metal ion (which would be geometrical or optical isomers).

Score:4/-1 4.00

Detailed Answer: Assertion is correct, reason is incorrect
Assertion:
Chain isomerism is indeed observed in compounds with four or more carbon atoms. This is because you need at least four carbons to create a branched chain, which is the basis of chain isomerism. For example, butane (C4H10) has two isomers: n-butane and isobutane.

The reason that only alkanes show chain isomerism is incorrect. While alkanes with four or more carbons can exhibit chain isomerism, so can other types of hydrocarbons like alkenes and alkynes, as long as they have four or more carbons.

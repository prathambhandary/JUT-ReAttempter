Question No:20

A wire of resistance 5 Ω is drawn out so that its length is increased to twice its original length. Find the new resistance of the wire.

1 ) 20 Ω 2 ) 40 Ω
3 ) 60 Ω 4 ) 80 Ω

Your Option: 1

Correct Option: 1

Score:4/-1 4.00

Detailed Answer: If a wire is stretched, its length L increases and its diameter and hence the cross-sectional area A decreases, but the volume of the wire remains the same.
Hence A L = constant. Thus
A 1 L 1 = A 2 L 2
Given L 2 = 2 L 1 .
Therefore A 2 = A 1 L 1 L 2 = A 1 2
Original resistance R 1 = ρ L 1 A 1
New resistance   R 2 = ρ L 2 A 2
Resistivity ρ depends only on the material of the wire and hence it remains the same.
R 2 R 1 = L 2 L 1 × A 1 A 2 = 2 × 2 = 4 ⇒ R 2 = 4 R 1 = 4 × 5 = 20 Ω

Question No:30

Given below are two statements:
Statement I: Numbering in a bridged bicyclic organic compound always commences at one of the bridgehead atoms and proceeds along the longest bridge first.
Statement II: For a bicyclic compound containing a principal functional group, the numbering always starts directly at the bridgehead atom closest to the functional group, completely bypassing the bridge length rules.
In the light of the above statements, choose the correct answer:

1 ) Statement I is true but Statement II is false. 2 ) Statement I is false but Statement II is true.
3 ) Both Statement I and Statement II are true. 4 ) Both Statement I and Statement II are false.

Your Option: 1

Correct Option: 1

Score:4/-1 4.00

Detailed Answer: Statement I is true: Bicyclic nomenclature dictates that you must start at a bridgehead carbon and count along the largest ring/bridge path to the second bridgehead carbon, followed by the second longest bridge, and finally the shortest bridge.
Statement II is false: Substituents and functional groups do not override the structural path constraint. The counting path must prioritize structural bridge size first (largest bridge -> medium bridge -> smallest bridge). Functional group locants are only optimized to be as low as possible within that set path constraints.

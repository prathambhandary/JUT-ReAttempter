Question No:1

The time period of a simple harmonic oscillator is T = 2 π k m . Measured value of mass ( m ) of the object is 10 g with an accuracy of 10 mg and time for 50 oscillations of the spring is found to be 60 s using a watch of 2 s resolution. Percentage error in determination of spring constant ( k ) is _ _ _ _ 1 % .

1 ) 7.60 2 ) 3.35
3 ) 3.43 4 ) 6.76

Your Option: 4

Correct Option: 4

Score:4/-1 4.00

Detailed Answer: The given formula for time period is
T = 2 π k m .   
Squaring both sides, we get T 2 = 4 π 2 k m , which gives k = 4 π 2 m T 2 .
The relative error in k is given by Δ k k = Δ m m + 2 Δ T T .
Given: m = 10   g , Δ m = 10 m g = 0.01   g .
Total time for n = 50 oscillations is t = 60   s with resolution Δ t = 2   s .
Since T = t / n , the relative error in T is the same as in t : Δ T T = Δ t t .
Substituting the values: Δ k k = 0.01 10 + 2 ( 2 60 ) .
Δ k k = 0.001 + 2 30 = 0.001 + 0.0666 …
Percentage error = 0.001 + 1 15 × 100 = 0.1 + 100 15 = 0.1 + 6.666 … = 6.766 … % .
Rounding to two decimal places
we get 6.76 % .

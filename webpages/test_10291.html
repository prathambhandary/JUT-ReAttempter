1 ) Increases with increase in temperature 2 ) Decreases with increase in temperature
3 ) Is independent of temperature 4 ) Changes suddenly at 400  K

Your Option: 2

Correct Option: 2

Score:4/-1 1.00

Detailed Answer: Relaxation time τ is the average time between two successive collisions of conduction electrons.
When temperature increases, the vibrations of the metal ions increase.
Hence, electrons undergo collisions more frequently, reducing their relaxation time.
τ ∝ 1 T
Therefore, the correct answer is:
Decreases with increase in temperature.

Question No:9

Consider the situation shown in fig. If the switch is closed and after some time it is opened again, the closed loop will show

1 ) an anticlockwise current-pulse 2 ) a clockwise current-pulse
3 ) an anticlockwise current-pulse and then a clockwise current-pulse 4 ) a clockwise current-pulse and then an anticlockwise current-pulse

Your Option: 4

Correct Option: 4

Score:4/-1 1.00

Detailed Answer: When the switch is closed: The current in wire AB increases, creating an increasing magnetic field in the loop. According to Lenz's Law, the loop induces a current in the clockwise direction to oppose this increase in the magnetic field.
When the switch is opened: The current in the circuit suddenly drops, causing a decrease in the magnetic field within the loop. To oppose this decrease, the induced current in the loop reverses, flowing in an anti-clockwise direction.

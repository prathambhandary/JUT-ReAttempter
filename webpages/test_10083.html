Your Option: 1

Correct Option: 1

Score:4/-1 4.00

Detailed Answer: Both the Assertion and the Reason are correct, and the Reason correctly explains the Assertion. During pachytene of prophase I, crossing over occurs between non-sister chromatids of homologous chromosomes. However, the points of exchange are not immediately visible because the homologous chromosomes remain tightly paired by the synaptonemal complex.

Why this exists

Built by Jnanasudha alumni, for Jnanasudha students

JUT ReAttempter is an independent, unofficial project created by alumni of Jnanasudha Institutions. The daily JUT test series gives students valuable questions and practice, but once a test has passed, students may want another opportunity to revise those questions under test conditions.

This tool turns previous JUTs into fresh randomized practice papers that can be used for both JEE Main and KCET preparation.

It isn't affiliated with Jnanasudha Institutions, the National Testing Agency, or the Karnataka Examinations Authority. It is simply an independent practice project built by former students for the students coming up behind them.

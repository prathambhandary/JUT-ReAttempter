How it works

Four steps from old papers to a new attempt

No two attempts are ever the same paper, even if you pick the exact same JUT numbers again.

01

Choose your JUT pool

Pick any combination of JUT test numbers you want your questions pulled from — one, a few, or all of them.

02

Get a fresh 75-Q paper

20 MCQs + 5 numericals each for Physics, Chemistry and Maths, shuffled at random from your chosen pool.

03

Attempt it, NTA-style

Subject tabs, question palette, mark-for-review, save & next, and a real 3-hour countdown — the CBT interface you already know.

04

Instant, verified scorecard

Your answers are graded on the server against the real answer key — subject-wise marks, accuracy, and full solutions.

Why this exists

Built by Jnanasudha alumni, for Jnanasudha students

JUT ReAttempter is an independent, unofficial project created by alumni of Jnanasudha Institutions. The college's daily JUT test series doesn't offer a way to reattempt an older test once the day has passed — so this tool rebuilds any past JUT into a fresh, randomized CBT mock whenever a student wants extra revision. It isn't affiliated with Jnanasudha Institutions or the NTA; it's simply a practice tool made by former students who wanted this option for the juniors coming up behind them.
